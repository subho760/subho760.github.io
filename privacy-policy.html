Privacy Policy

This is a general privacy policy for the mobile application that links to this page. It is designed to cover advertising providers that may be configured in different releases. It does not mean every listed provider is active in every version.

The App may process information needed to provide its features, maintain settings, diagnose errors, protect the service, or respond to actions you take. Depending on the App's features and configuration, some processing may happen on your device and some may involve online services. Please do not enter information into the App that you do not want processed for the feature you are using.

The information handled depends on the features enabled in the particular App and release. The developer should ensure this description matches the App's actual behaviour and any services it connects to.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The App may use third-party advertising or mediation services to request, display, measure, and protect advertisements. Depending on the configuration of a particular release, these services may include:

  • Google services / Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads
  • Meta Audience Network
  • AppLovin
  • Unity LevelPlay mediation
  • Other advertising or mediation providers added to a future release.

Only providers actually enabled or used by the installed release should be treated as active. Listing a provider here does not mean it is currently integrated, enabled, or receiving information. Advertising providers and their partners may process information such as advertising or device identifiers, IP address, app and device details, approximate location inferred from network information, diagnostics, and ad request, impression, or interaction events, depending on the provider and its settings.

These third parties may process information under their own privacy policies. Review the linked policies above and the privacy information supplied by any additional provider enabled in a future release.

In-App Purchases and Payments

The App may offer optional in-app purchases, paid features, or subscriptions. Purchases may be processed through the app store or payment provider used to distribute the App, such as Google Play.

The payment provider processes payment information in accordance with its own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not receive or store full payment card details through the App unless explicitly stated otherwise.

The App may receive or process limited purchase-related information, such as purchase status, product type, transaction identifiers, or subscription status, where necessary to unlock purchased features, verify purchases, prevent fraud, or provide customer support.

Purchase availability, payment processing, refunds, cancellations, and subscription management are subject to the relevant app store or payment provider's policies and procedures.

Information may be processed by service providers when needed to operate App features, deliver advertising, prevent abuse, measure performance, or meet legal obligations.
