Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Easy Unit Converter handles information. Review it whenever app features or third-party services change.

1. Converter and on-device settings

Unit conversions and calculator expressions are processed on your device. Preferences such as theme and decimal precision may be stored locally. The app does not require an account for its core tools.

2. Advertising and third-party services

Depending on the configuration of a particular release, the app may use Unity Ads, Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Unity LevelPlay, or another advertising provider selected by the developer. Only providers actually enabled in that release are active; naming a provider here does not mean it is active in every build.

When advertising is requested, the active provider and its partners may process information such as advertising or device identifiers, IP address, app/device technical information, diagnostics, and ad delivery, impression, or interaction events. Data handling, personalization, retention, and available controls depend on the provider, region, and device settings.

3. In-app purchases

If a release offers in-app purchases, purchases are handled by the app store or payment service shown during checkout. The app developer does not receive or store your full payment-card details through the app. The store may process transaction, account, and purchase-status information to complete purchases, prevent fraud, and support refunds or restore-purchase features.

4. Network access and core tools

Core conversion and calculator functions are designed to work locally. Advertising, store purchases, and opening external policy links may require network access. If an ad cannot load, core tools should remain available.

5. Children’s privacy

The app is not intended to collect personal details directly from children. Advertising, purchase, and age-related settings must be configured in accordance with applicable law and store requirements.

6. Changes and contact

This policy does not use a fixed expiry date. It should be reviewed and updated whenever the app’s data practices, features, or enabled service providers change. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the app’s store listing or developer profile.
